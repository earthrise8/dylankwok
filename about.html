About Me

Creator, Engineer, and STEM Educator

Who I Am

I'm Dylan Kwok, a passionate creator and innovator based in the East Bay, California. I'm driven by a curiosity about how things work and a deep commitment to sharing that passion with others through education and hands-on projects.

My work spans engineering, photography, and STEM education. Whether I'm designing rockets, capturing moments through a lens, or mentoring students, I approach every project with the same spirit of curiosity and excellence.

Background

I'm a student at Irvington High School in Fremont, CA, where I maintain a 3.73 unweighted GPA while pursuing honors coursework in mathematics and sciences. My academic foundation has been essential to my success in engineering competitions and research projects.

Beyond the classroom, I'm most excited about practical problem-solving and building things that make a difference. This has led me to lead robotics teams, mentor younger students, and undertake ambitious projects like high-power rocket design.

What I'm Passionate About

Aerospace Engineering

High-power rocketry has become a defining interest. I've led teams through competition cycles, designed modular systems, and worked toward Level 1 and Level 2 rocketry certifications. There's something magical about launching something you've built from concept to reality.

Photography

I capture the world through photography—from urban architecture to natural landscapes. Photography teaches me about composition, light, and perspective in ways that inform all my creative work.

STEM Education

I believe education should be hands-on and engaging. I've taught robotics and coding to middle school students, and I'm committed to making engineering accessible and exciting to the next generation.

Making & DIY

From 3D printing to woodworking, I love bringing ideas to life. The making community has taught me that creativity isn't limited to one discipline—it's an approach to problem-solving.

My Philosophy

I believe that learning is a lifelong journey. Every project teaches me something new, and every failure is an opportunity to improve. I approach challenges with creativity, persistence, and a willingness to ask "what if?"

I'm also passionate about sharing knowledge. Teaching others has been one of the most rewarding experiences of my life—there's nothing quite like the moment when a student understands a concept or completes their first project.

Looking forward, I want to pursue a career in aerospace or mechanical engineering where I can combine my technical skills with my passion for innovation and education.

Let's Connect

I'm always interested in collaborating on projects, discussing ideas, or mentoring students. Feel free to reach out!
